Cookie Policy

What cookies are used and how consent choices affect them.

Last updated: 27 February 2026

UK Cookie Position

For UK users, we follow UK PECR and UK GDPR principles. We only use optional cookies if you consent.

Necessary Cookies

These cookies are needed for login, security, and core app features. Without them, the signed-in experience will not work.

Optional Cookies

Optional cookies are for preference and analytics-style features. We only set them if you choose Accept all.

  • analytics_id (optional)
  • ui_locale (optional)

Consent Choices

  • Accept all: necessary + optional cookies enabled.
  • Accept necessary: only necessary cookies are kept.
  • Reject all: optional cookies are removed; necessary cookies are kept.

Change Your Choice

You can change your choice later by clearing cookies in your browser and reloading the app.
