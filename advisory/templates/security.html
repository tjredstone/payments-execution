Security

How your data is protected.

Last updated: 27 February 2026

How Bank Connections Work

Bank connections are established using Open Banking via TrueLayer, an FCA-regulated provider in the United Kingdom.

When you connect your bank account, you are redirected to your bank's secure authentication page. This application does not collect or store your online banking credentials.

Access is granted only with your explicit consent.

The connection is read-only and limited to retrieving account balances, transactions, direct debits, and standing orders required for advisory analysis. The application cannot initiate payments or move funds.

Open Banking access is granted for a limited period and must be re-authorised periodically in line with regulatory requirements. You can revoke access at any time through your bank or Open Banking provider.

Account Protection

Access to your data requires sign-in. Each user account is isolated using a unique user ID, ensuring one user cannot view or access another user's data.

Sign-in is protected with CSRF safeguards and login rate limits to reduce automated abuse.

Bank Tokens & Application Secrets

Bank access tokens are encrypted before being stored in the database.

Application secrets (including encryption keys and API credentials) are not hardcoded in the application. They are stored securely using environment-based secrets and/or secure system key storage.

Data in Transit

Database connections are configured to use TLS (SSL) encryption.

In hosted environments, HTTPS and secure cookies are enforced when enabled in deployment configuration.

Data at Rest

Data stored in the database is encrypted at rest by the database provider. Sensitive fields, including bank access tokens, are encrypted before storage at the application layer.

Only the minimum data required for advisory functionality is retained.

Older raw payload fields are scrubbed automatically, and records are deleted according to defined retention policies to reduce unnecessary data storage.

Logging & Exposure Controls

Application logs apply automatic redaction filters to sensitive values such as access tokens, refresh tokens, authentication codes, and client secrets.

This reduces the risk of accidental exposure through logging systems.

Your Data Rights

You can request access to your stored data and request deletion of your account data.

For UK users, this supports core UK GDPR rights, including access and erasure.

Important Limits

No online system can be completely risk-free.

We follow least-privilege access principles and fail-closed design wherever possible and continuously improve safeguards as the system evolves.
