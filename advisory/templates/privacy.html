Rent Advisory

Privacy Policy

How this service collects, uses, stores, and deletes your data.

Last updated: 27 February 2026

Who We Are

Rent Advisory is a payment guidance prototype. If you are in the UK, we handle personal data under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Data We Collect

When you connect your bank, we receive account, balance, transaction, direct debit, and standing order data. We also store the advisories this app generates.

How We Use Data

We use this data to forecast cash flow, spot upcoming obligations, and suggest when to pay. We do not sell your personal data.

In the UK, our main legal basis is that processing is needed to provide this service, plus legitimate interests for operating and securing it.

Security

We protect sensitive values with encryption, secure secret storage, and access controls. Older data is deleted or scrubbed by retention rules.

Retention

We keep data only as long as needed for advisory operation and testing. After that, it is automatically deleted or reduced.

Your Controls

You can disconnect your bank link and request deletion of your stored data. UK users may also request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, and portability where applicable.

Complaints

If you are in the UK and think we handled your data badly, you can contact the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.
